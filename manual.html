1. Getting started

The home screen has:

  • Scoreboard: score a bout on this device. It works fully offline.
  • Fencing Live: host an event, referee it, or follow it live. It needs internet.
  • 📷 Scan QR code: scan any Starport code you're given (event, check-in, referee, host or scoreboard screen) and the right screen opens straight away. You can also scan with the phone's own camera app.

Use it in a browser at portusstellaelab.github.io/starportfencing, add it to your home screen, or install the Android app from Google Play. Everything works the same on each.

The ⚙ on the home screen holds sound, vibration and épée settings.

2. Scoreboard

Setting up a bout

  1. Choose the weapon (foil, épée, sabre) and format: Pool (5 touches, 3 minutes), Veteran DE (10), Senior DE (15, three periods) or Team relay (45, nine bouts).
  2. Enter both names, with country and club if you like.
  3. Tap Start match.

Scoring

  • Tap a fencer's side to give them a touch. The timer pauses automatically.
  • Undo takes back the last touch or card for that fencer.
  • Cards: yellow and red (a red gives the opponent a touch), with a full-screen flash so everyone sees the call.
  • Épée: the double-touch button scores both fencers at once. The optional passivity timer (⚙ settings) runs a one-minute no-touch clock and prompts for P-cards (P-red then P-black from 1 Sep 2026, or the earlier P-yellow ladder if you turn it on).
  • Sabre DE: two halves with a break when a fencer reaches half the target, instead of timed periods.
  • Video review: one review per fencer per bout, kept when a call is overturned.
  • ⇄ between the names swaps the sides on screen to match where the fencers stand.

Timer

  • The large button starts and pauses the clock.
  • Set time changes the clock; Reset puts it back to the start of the period.
  • Tie break sets one minute; Break starts the one-minute break between periods.

Saving and sharing

  • When the bout ends, save it. Saved bouts keep a full timeline of every touch with the clock reading, plus your notes.
  • Share a result card as a picture, or send the bout link to the fencers.
  • If the phone or app closes mid-bout, the bout is saved automatically and offered again when you reopen the app.

3. Team relays on the scoreboard

  • A relay is nine bouts to 45, in the FIE order. The bout list under the timer shows every bout; tap one to review or correct it.
  • ⇄ Team order swaps fencers within a team (for example a substitute coming in).
  • The match total always adds up from the individual bouts.

4. Big-screen scoreboard 📺

Turn an iPad, laptop or TV into a classic scoreboard by the piste (on the floor, or on top of the scoring lights) while the referee scores on their phone.

  1. On the referee's phone, open the bout in the scoreboard and tap 📺 at the top, then Create screen code.
  2. On the screen device, tap 📷 Scan QR code on the home screen and scan the QR the phone shows, or open Fencing Live → Scoreboard screen and type the 6-character code.
  3. Tap Full screen on the screen device.

The screen shows names, clubs, the score, the timer, period or relay bout, cards and breaks, live. It has no buttons to press, so it's safe to leave by the piste. The phone keeps the same code for every bout it scores, so the screen stays at that piste all day. Tap 📺 → Stop this screen when you're finished.

Both devices need internet. The screen runs the clock itself between updates, so it uses very little data.

5. Hosting an event (Fencing Live)

  1. Go to Fencing Live → Create new event.
  2. Choose Teams or Individual. For individual events, choose the format:
    • Pool: round-robin pools only.
    • DE: a direct-elimination tableau only, seeded by skill.
    • Pool + DE: pools first, then a tableau seeded from the pool results. This is the usual competition format.
    For DE, choose Senior (15) or Veteran (10).
  3. Add the date, start time, venue and weapon, then Create.
  4. Write down the event code and host PIN it shows you. You need them to open the event on another device.
Publish: changes you make stay on your device until you tap ⬆ Publish in the bar at the top. Then fencers, referees and spectators see them.

⚙ in the event opens Event settings: name, date, time, venue, weapon and (for individual events) the format. Manage my events on the Fencing Live page deletes old or test events. Every event deletes itself 7 days after its date.

6. Fencers and registration

Adding fencers yourself

  • + Add fencer: name, country, club and skill level (1 beginner to 5 national level). Skill is used to make even teams and pools.
  • Paste a list: one fencer per line, as Name, Club, Skill.

Letting fencers and referees register themselves

  1. In the Share tab, tap Open registration (or set times, below).
  2. Share the Fencers & viewers QR or link. People tap Register to fence or Register as referee and fill in the same details you would.
  3. New registrations appear at the top of the Fencers tab (and the Referees tab for referees). Check them, then tap Add or Add all, or ✕ to remove one. Then Publish.

Registration is closed until you open it. Each person sees on their phone when they've been added.

Scheduling registration and check-in

In the Share tab, tap ⏰ Set times and choose when registration and check-in open and close. They open and close by themselves at those times, and the times are printed on the event info sheet. Leave a box empty to use the buttons instead. Closing by hand always wins.

7. Teams and reserves

  • In the Teams tab, choose 3 or 4 fencers per team (or Manual) and tap Build teams by skill. Teams come out balanced; you can move anyone by tapping them, then Move here on another team.
  • With teams of 4, the fourth fencer is the reserve (R). Choose whether reserves fence:
    • Substitute only: fencers 1–3 fence the FIE order and the reserve only comes in as a substitute.
    • All 4 fence: the highest-skill fencer opens, closes and fences 3 bouts; the others fence 2 each, ordered by skill, and nobody fences two bouts in a row.
  • Late arrivals and walk-ins: add them with + Add fencer at any time, even after check-in has closed; they're checked in automatically when you Publish. You can put them in any team, even one that already has 4: extra people are reserves, and the referee chooses who fences from the score sheet.
  • Changing a team, or the reserve setting, updates every match that hasn't started when you Publish. Matches already started or finished keep their line-up.
  • The same reserve switch is on the Schedule tab. To change just one match, open it and use ⇄ Change fencers → Reserves in this match.

8. Check-in

  1. Publish your fencers and teams first.
  2. When doors open, tap Open check-in in the Share tab (or let your scheduled time open it).
  3. Fencers scan the Fencers & viewers QR (with 📷 Scan QR code on the home screen or their camera app) and tap their name. You see arrivals live in the Fencers tab; tap a name to check someone in by hand.
  4. Close check-in, then tap See no-shows. Mark them absent: they're taken out of their teams and draws. Adjust the teams, rebuild the draw if needed, and Publish.

9. Schedule: pools, DE and rounds

Individual events

  • ⚡ Generate pools: choose the number of pools; fencers are spread by skill, bouts are to 5 in the FIE order, one pool per piste.
  • ⚡ Build Round 1 (DE): builds the tableau. In Pool + DE events it's seeded from the pool results (wins ÷ bouts, then indicator, then touches scored). Wait until every pool bout is finished; the app warns you if some aren't.
  • Press the round button again as results come in to add the next round, up to the final.

Team events

  • ⚡ Build Round 1 suggests the pairings; everything is editable. Byes go to the top seeds.
  • Build each next round when results are in. 🏆 Podium sets gold, silver and bronze.

Working with matches

  • ⇆ Spread over pistes spreads a round across your pistes so nobody fences twice in a row.
  • + Add match by hand for anything extra, including Final and Bronze.
  • Tap any match to choose: Use the built-in scoreboard on this device, Enter the score (when the venue has its own scoreboard or a paper sheet), Change fencers (teams) or Edit match.
  • Show the 9-bout order lists who fences each relay bout.
  • ↺ Reset generated draw removes generated matches that have no results.

10. Entering scores and results

  • Scores reach the standings the moment they're entered, from the built-in scoreboard, a referee's phone or the host's score sheet, so you can build the next round straight away.
  • Score sheet: for relays, type each bout's touches; the running relay score is worked out for you. Set the status to Final when the match is over.
  • Results tab: standings, with ties shown as "3=". Tap a match to correct it.
  • A match stuck In progress (opened by mistake)? Tap it and choose ↺ Not started — back to upcoming, or 🔓 Unlock to keep the score and let another referee carry on.
  • Lock the results when the event is over so nobody can change them.
  • Share or print results as a picture or PDF, with or without the bout details.

11. Share tab: QR codes and printouts

  • Registration and Check-in cards with their open/close buttons and times.
  • Three QR codes:
    • Fencers & viewers: follow live, register, check in.
    • Referees: opens the referee screen with the PIN filled in. Only show it to referees.
    • Host: opens the event with full control on a committee member's phone. Keep it private.
  • Print: event info (with the QR, registration and check-in times), check-in sheet, teams, bout schedule, results, referee card and host card. Each prints as a PDF across as many pages as it needs.

12. Referees

  1. Scan the Referees QR with 📷 Scan QR code on the home screen (or use Fencing Live → Referee an event with the code and referee PIN) and pick your name. If you registered, your name is in the list.
  2. Team matches: confirm the line-up first. Before you start, the app asks you to check each team's positions against the paper score sheet: 1, 2, 3 (and 4, 5, 6) and R. Pick anyone in the team, or choose Someone else… and type a name. The 9 bouts then follow the FIE score-sheet order, and the reserve sits out. Tap Skip to keep the order as it is.
  3. Reserve comes in: in ⇄ Change fencers, choose who the reserve replaces and from which bout, then Swap in. A note (e.g. "B1 (R) replaced C1 from bout 5") is saved with the match for everyone to see.
  4. Tap your bout. Choose Referee with the built-in scoreboard, or Enter the score if the venue has its own scoreboard or a paper sheet.
  5. While you score, the bout is locked to you so nobody else scores it at the same time.
  6. Need to swap? Tap 🤝 Hand over: the score so far is uploaded and the next referee carries on from it.
  7. Opened the wrong bout? Go back and choose not to save. If nothing was scored, the bout goes back to upcoming and is unlocked.
  8. Teams: ⇄ Change fencers on the day if a team changes its order. If someone who isn't in the team fences for them, use Someone not in the team fencing for them?: choose who they replace, type their name and tap Use. It only changes that match, and everyone sees the new name.
  9. The line under the score shows the upload: ✓ uploaded, or ⚠ not uploaded with the reason. Scores that didn't go through are kept on the phone and retried by themselves every 30 seconds and as soon as the internet is back; you can also tap the line to retry.

13. Fencers and spectators

  • Scan the event QR with 📷 Scan QR code on the home screen, or go to Fencing Live and enter the event code. No login.
  • Find me highlights your bouts and shows your next one; star your team in the standings.
  • Register to fence while registration is open, and Check in when you arrive.
  • Bouts: live scores and the schedule by piste. Turn the 9-bout details on or off.
  • Tableau: the DE bracket or team rounds, with byes, scores and winners, and the podium at the end. Tap a match for its details.
  • Standings and Teams. In Teams, R marks each team's reserve, with a note on whether reserves fence in this event.

14. Fixing common problems

ProblemWhat to do
Viewers don't see my changesTap ⬆ Publish. The bar says "Everyone sees the latest version" when it's done.
A match says In progress but nobody's fencingHost: tap it → Not started — back to upcoming.
A referee is locked out ("someone is refereeing this bout")Ask them to tap 🤝 Hand over, or the host unlocks it from the match.
Someone turned up late or didn't registerFencers tab → + Add fencer, put them in a team (even a full one), Publish. They're checked in automatically.
The score sheet's 1·2·3·R differ from the appReferee: tap the match → confirm the line-up (or ⇄ Change fencers → Line-up from the score sheet).
A fencer didn't comeMark them absent (Fencers tab), move people between teams, then Publish. Matches not yet started update.
Registration form says it's closedCheck the times under ⏰ Set times; a close time ends registration even if you opened it.
Lost the host PINOpen the event on the device that created it (Fencing Live → Open my event), or contact us.
A referee's score shows "not uploaded"Nothing is lost: it's kept on their phone and retried by itself. The message says why (no internet, PIN, results locked, too many requests). If it's urgent, the host taps the match → Enter the score.
No internet at the venueThe Scoreboard works offline. Fencing Live needs internet; scores are kept on the phone and sent when it's back.
