Starport Fencing is a free scoreboard and live-results app with no ads, built and funded by Sammy, a fencer representing Australia. We partner with brands and services that look after athletes.

Who you'd be partnering with

I'm Sammy, a fencer representing Australia, balancing work, study and intensive training while chasing my goals on the international stage. I built Starport Fencing after running team competitions at the University of Melbourne: we had wireless scoring boxes but no scoreboards, so every score, bout order and result was tracked by hand. The app takes that manual work away, and I've kept it free for every club and fencer.

The app scores bouts, runs club nights and competitions (pools, DE, team events, registration and check-in), and puts live scores on every phone and on a big screen by the piste.

Who you'd reach

Fencing clubs, competitors, referees, coaches and parents who use the app at training and events, plus the wider combat-sport community: BJJ, MMA and more. It's an engaged, active audience that cares about performance, recovery and gear.

The bigger picture

Support also goes towards athletes facing hardship: competition and training fees, travel, gear, injury and mental health support. The long-term goal is a not-for-profit National Combat Athletes Academy with world-class training and programs for athletes living with neurodivergence, mental health conditions or financial distress. Partners are part of that story from the start.

The app itself stays free and ad-free. Partners are thanked here and on Ko-fi, never with ads inside the app.
