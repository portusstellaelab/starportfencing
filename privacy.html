Privacy

Starport Fencing · Portus Stellae Lab · Last updated 29 October 2026
In short: no ads, no tracking, no analytics, and nothing is sold. The Scoreboard works entirely on your device. Fencing Live stores event information on our server only so everyone at the event can see it, and deletes it automatically after the event.

Scoreboard

Bouts you score, saved matches, fencer names you type and your settings are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us unless you choose to share or upload them (for example, sending a result to a Fencing Live event).

Fencing Live

When an organiser hosts an event, the information they enter is stored on our server (run on Cloudflare) so fencers, referees and spectators can follow it:

  • event details (name, date, venue), fencer and team names, clubs, skill levels, bout orders, scores, check-ins and referee names;
  • host and referee PINs, which are never shown publicly.

When an organiser opens registration, fencers and referees can register themselves with the same details (name, club, country, skill level for fencers). Registrations are stored with the event until the organiser adds or removes them.

Anyone with the event code can view the public event page. Organisers are responsible for having permission to list the people in their event, including parental permission for junior fencers.

Events are deleted automatically 7 days after the event date, or earlier if the organiser deletes them. To have an event removed sooner, the organiser can delete it in the app or contact us.

Scoreboard screens

If a referee shows their bout on a big screen, the names, clubs, scores, timer and cards of that bout are sent to our server so the screen can show them. Nothing else is kept, and a screen’s data is deleted when the referee stops it or after 12 hours without use.

Feedback

If you send feedback from the app, we receive your message, the type (idea, problem or other), any name or email you choose to add, the event code if you tick it, the app version, the type of device and your internet address. We use it only to reply and to improve the app, and delete it when it is no longer needed.

Member programs and member cards

If you apply to join a member program (such as the Starport Pioneers Program), we receive your name, email, mobile (if you add it), where you live, whether you live in Australia permanently or long-term, your sport, role and club, the type of phone you use, and what you tell us about yourself. We use these details only to run that program, choose members, invite testers and contact you. Your email may be added to the Google Play tester list if you join the testing round.

Members get a card code. Anyone with your card code or card QR can see your first name and last initial, the program, member number, sport and role, and whether the card is valid, so share it only when you use an offer. Ask us at any time to remove your application or card.

Donations

Donations are made on Ko-fi and paid through Stripe or PayPal. We never see your card details. Ko-fi tells us your name or display name, the amount and any message you leave. Ko-fi also tells our server the amount and date of each donation, so the app can show how much each appeal has raised. Our server keeps the amount, date, Ko-fi reference and any message you leave, so we can use your donation for what you asked. It doesn't keep your name or email. Messages are seen only by us, and the app shows only totals. See Ko-fi’s privacy policy.

Camera and microphone

The camera is used when you tap “Scan QR code” or “Film”. A QR image is read on your device and never saved or sent. When you film a bout, the video (and sound, if you allow the microphone) is recorded only on your device and is saved or shared only when you choose to. Nothing you film is uploaded to us.

Internet addresses and abuse protection

To keep Fencing Live free and working, our server counts requests per internet address to stop automated abuse. These counts are kept for about an hour. Daily totals with no addresses are kept for 14 days. Addresses blocked for abuse are kept until the block ends.

Sharing

We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for advertising. Data is processed by Cloudflare (hosting) and, for donations, Ko-fi and its payment providers. Data may be stored outside Australia by these providers.

Your choices

  • Delete saved matches and settings by clearing them in the app or uninstalling it.
  • Ask us to see, correct or delete information about you, including feedback or an entry in an event.
